# What I Am Working On Now

This page describes current public-facing ArcTopia workstreams. It intentionally excludes protected implementation details and confidential empirical material.

## Decision continuity & revalidation

When can an earlier conclusion remain usable, when do changed conditions require re-evaluation, and when does its earlier basis no longer support consequential action?

**Current stage:** systems architecture and experimental testing.  
**Evidence status:** validation not claimed.

## Trajectory-sensitive systems

Can apparently equivalent present states require different evaluation because their transformation histories differ?

Bounded demonstrations test whether pathway information can matter beyond a specified present-state description.

**Current stage:** technical demonstration and controlled testing.  
**Evidence status:** feasibility demonstrated in bounded cases; empirical value remains under test.

## Evidence, authority & execution

How should systems distinguish what evidence supports, who is authorized to decide, and whether that authorization remains actionable when execution occurs?

**Current stage:** systems architecture and formalization.

## Meaningful Human Control

When does formal human authority remain practically exercisable in AI-mediated decisions rather than merely present on paper?

Current work examines competence, independent evidence, intervention, challenge, fallback, reconsideration, and revalidation.

**Current stage:** manuscript revision, research programme, and bounded testing.

## Future Capability Preservation

How does transformation change future agency, alternatives, recovery capacity, adaptability, and room to manoeuvre?

**Current stage:** two public theoretical papers plus empirical protocol development.  
**Evidence status:** empirical validation pending.

## Prospective and controlled validation

How can theory be tested without defining success after outcomes are already known?

Current work uses frozen definitions, bounded evidence rules, credible comparators, and tests that allow null or adverse outcomes.

**Current stage:** protocol development and evidence collection.  
**Evidence status:** results are not claimed here unless they have been separately reviewed for public release.

## Disclosure boundary

Public descriptions identify the research question, capability-level requirements, evidence status, and non-enabling demonstrations.

Protected representations, algorithms, schemas, activation mechanisms, private case records, and implementation details remain outside this repository.
