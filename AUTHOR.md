# Author

## Linda Thorstensen

Linda Thorstensen is an independent researcher and systems architect working across AI-mediated decision systems, consequential transformation, verification, meaningful human control, future capability, and long-horizon institutional decision-making.

Her public research includes:

- Future Capability Preservation;
- decision-time verification and revalidation;
- meaningful human control in AI-mediated institutions;
- transformation memory, provenance, and reconstructability;
- trajectory-sensitive evaluation;
- legitimacy, contestability, and continuity through change;
- empirical testing of capability- and pathway-sensitive propositions.

ArcTopia is an original research programme developed and directed by Linda Thorstensen. ArcTopia-specific formulations, terminology, architectures, research materials, and public research directions released under her name are presented as her work. This does not assert ownership over established fields, general ideas, or prior art.

This repository contains only material deliberately approved for public sharing. Private research, source code, technical mechanisms, datasets, formal specifications, and implementation architecture are maintained separately.
