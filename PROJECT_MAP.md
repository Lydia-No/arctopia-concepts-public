> How should consequential decisions remain valid, challengeable, and adaptable as conditions change?

## Future Capability Preservation

Examines how consequential transformation changes the capabilities and meaningful possibilities that remain available later.

Public questions include retained options, competence, reversibility, repair, reconstructability, adaptation, and generation of alternatives.

## Decision-Time Verification & Human Control

Examines whether previously established approval or authority remains practically meaningful at the point of consequential action.

Public questions include retained competence, evidence access, intervention, challenge, fallback, reconsideration, and the conditions that trigger revalidation.

## Transformation Memory, Provenance & Recovery

Examines what must remain traceable when present states alone are insufficient to evaluate what may legitimately or safely happen next.

Public questions include pathway information, reconstructability, provenance, recovery, and same-present-state cases with materially different histories.

## Cross-cutting work

Across the programme, current public-facing work also examines:

- decision continuity and revalidation;
- trajectory-sensitive evaluation;
- evidence, authority, and execution;
- legitimacy and contestability through change;
- prospective and controlled empirical testing.

## What this map does not show

This map is a public research orientation. It does not disclose protected representations, algorithms, schemas, activation mechanisms, private system topology, or implementation logic.
