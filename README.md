# ArcTopia — Public Research & Concepts

ArcTopia is an independent research programme by Linda Thorstensen concerned with consequential transformation, decision validity through change, future capability, and meaningful human control.

The central question is:

> What remains possible after change?

ArcTopia develops research, systems architecture, bounded technical demonstrations, and empirical tests for decisions whose evidential, authorization, or capability conditions can change over time.

This repository is a deliberately limited public record. It presents selected research concepts and evidence status without publishing protected implementation mechanisms.

## Public research areas

### Future Capability Preservation

How does transformation reshape the meaningful capabilities and possibilities that remain available later?

This work examines retained options, competence, adaptability, reversibility, repair capacity, reconstructability, and the ability to generate alternatives under changing conditions.

### Decision-Time Verification & Human Control

When is previously established authority still actionable when a consequential decision is actually executed?

This work distinguishes formal approval from practical control and examines whether evidence, competence, intervention, challenge, fallback, and reconsideration remain genuinely available.

### Transformation Memory, Provenance & Recovery

How can systems preserve enough history to distinguish present states that look similar but permit different next actions?

This work examines transformation history, provenance, reconstructability, and the conditions required for later revalidation, challenge, recovery, and alternative action.

## What is included here

This repository may contain:

- public research questions and conceptual definitions;
- high-level relationships among the public research areas;
- non-enabling diagrams and explanatory notes;
- verified links to already-public papers;
- public research status and evidence limits;
- authorship and citation information.

## What is not included here

This repository intentionally excludes:

- private source code, tests, schemas, APIs, or deployment material;
- protected algorithms, activation mechanisms, thresholds, weights, decision tables, or rule composition;
- private system architecture sufficient to reproduce implementation;
- confidential datasets, pilots, evidence records, operational logs, or internal research archives;
- unpublished patent claims, invention disclosures, novelty analyses, or legal correspondence;
- claim-complete descriptions of potentially protectable technical combinations.

Implementation feasibility, theoretical support, publication status, and empirical validation are treated as different things and are labelled separately.
