# ArcTopia — Public Repository Boundaries

This document defines what this public repository does and does not represent.

## 1. Public conceptual scope

The repository may describe research questions, conceptual distinctions, evidence status, public papers, high-level system requirements, and non-enabling examples.

It does not constitute a complete ArcTopia model, ontology, implementation specification, or operational protocol.

## 2. Implementation boundary

Protected implementation details are excluded, including:

- algorithms and executable decision logic;
- thresholds, weights, scoring functions, and activation conditions;
- state-transition or rule-composition mechanisms;
- internal schemas, APIs, data models, or deployment architecture;
- private verification, revalidation, lineage, propagation, or counterfactual mechanisms.

## 3. Authority boundary

ArcTopia research concerns evaluation, verification, decision support, and the conditions under which authority remains meaningful.

A computational system does not acquire legal, institutional, or normative authority merely by producing a measurement, recommendation, or model output.

Authority remains grounded in accountable human and institutional processes.

## 4. Evidence boundary

A conceptual proposition, public paper, implemented demonstrator, and empirical result are different kinds of evidence.

This repository does not treat implementation as validation or publication as proof of a general claim.

## 5. Simulation and recorded history

Counterfactual and simulated analysis may support reasoning, but hypothetical states should remain distinguishable from recorded historical evidence.

## 6. Public/private separation

Private research archives, technical architecture, protected methods, confidential cases, operational evidence, and unpublished formal work remain outside this repository unless separately reviewed and intentionally released.

These boundaries are deliberate.
